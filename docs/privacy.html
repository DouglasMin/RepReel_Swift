Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 5, 2026

Welcome to RepReel ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how our iOS application collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use RepReel.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect the minimal information necessary to deliver our workout tracking and AI parsing services:

  • Account Information: If you use Sign in with Apple or email authentication, we receive your randomized user identifier or email address to sync your workout routines across devices.
  • User-Shared Links: When you share an Instagram Reel or YouTube Short URL via the iOS Share Sheet, the link is processed by our secure backend to extract workout details (exercise names, sets, reps, rest intervals).
  • Workout & Fitness Logs: Workout session history, recorded weights, reps completed, and session timestamps entered by you.
  • Device & Usage Data: Non-personally identifiable diagnostic information and crash logs used strictly to improve app reliability.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data solely for the following purposes:

  • To parse, organize, and present structured workout programs from shared video links.
  • To synchronize your routines, completed sessions, and volume analytics across your Apple devices.
  • To provide automated rest timers and live workout tracking.
  • To diagnose technical errors and ensure service stability.

3. Third-Party AI & Cloud Services

To convert social media video links into structured routines, RepReel securely communicates with serverless AI models (such as Google Gemini). Only the public video URL and public caption text are transmitted for transcription. No personal identifying details, financial information, or private fitness logs are shared with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Data Storage and Security

All communications between the RepReel client and our backend servers are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Workout data stored in the cloud is secured with industry-standard encryption protocols and strict access controls.

5. Data Retention & Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Deletion: You can delete individual routines or workout logs directly within the app at any time.
  • Account Deletion: You may request complete deletion of your account and all associated workout history by emailing us at min990214@gmail.com. All data will be permanently purged within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

RepReel is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
